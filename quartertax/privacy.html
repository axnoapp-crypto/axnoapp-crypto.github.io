QuarterTax Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-08

Summary

QuarterTax (the "Service") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Service handles and why.

1. What the Service is

The Service shows the four estimated tax payment due dates on a calendar, lets you note an amount for each quarter and mark it paid, keeps a payment log, and reminds you on your device before each date. There is no account, no sign-in, no link to the IRS or any payment service, and the developer runs no server that receives your information.

2. Information processed

Advertising identifiers Showing ads

Google AdMob uses the advertising ID and App Set ID to show ads. The developer does not collect or store these values.

Device and usage data Ad measurement and diagnostics

Ad impressions and clicks, and error and performance information, may be processed by Google AdMob. Nothing you type into the Service is included.

3. Information stored only on your device

The following stays in the Service's private storage on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • For each quarter you use: the amount you noted, the amount paid, the date paid, and an optional short note
  • Your reminder preferences

If you export a backup, it is saved as a file in the location you choose and is not sent to the developer.

4. Retention and deletion

Information on your device is deleted when you clear a quarter or everything in the Service, uninstall it, or clear its data in system settings. The developer keeps no copy.

5. Third-party services

Google AdMob Ads

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

6. Permissions

Internet Required

Used to show ads and to download a small public table of due dates from GitHub Pages once a day. That request carries none of the information you entered; like any web request, the server that answers it can see your IP address.

Notifications Optional

Requested when you save your first amount or turn reminders on. Used to remind you on your device before each due date. Everything else works without it.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process children's personal information.

8. Your choices

You can clear any quarter or everything in the Service, or uninstall it, to remove everything stored on your device. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email us and we will reply promptly.

  • Email: axno.app@gmail.com

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its date.
