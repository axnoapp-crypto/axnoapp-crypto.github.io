Gig Trip Costs Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-08

Summary

Gig Trip Costs (the "Service") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Service handles and why.

1. What the Service is

The Service is a notebook that keeps the trip receipts you type in — trip names, dates, line names, categories and yen amounts — on your own device. It adds up the amounts you enter; it does not connect to ticket sites, transit, hotels, banks or any account, and it cannot look up prices. It is an unofficial app, not affiliated with any artist, venue, ticket service or travel company, and it is not tax, financial or travel advice. There is no sign-up, no login and no identity check, and the developer operates no server that receives or stores your information.

2. Information handled

Advertising ID Showing ads

Google AdMob uses the advertising ID and App Set ID to show ads. The developer does not collect or store these values.

Device and usage information Ad measurement and diagnostics

Google AdMob may process ad impressions and clicks, and error and performance information. What you type into the Service is not included.

3. Information stored only on your device

The following is stored only in the app storage on your device and is never sent outside it.

  • The trip receipts you enter: names, dates, lines (category, name, amount) and whether a receipt is closed
  • Your reminder setting (on or off)

If you export a backup, it is saved as a file in the location you choose and is not sent to the developer.

4. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is erased immediately when you uninstall the Service or choose Settings > Apps > Storage > Clear data. The developer holds no other copy of your information.

5. External services

Google AdMob Serving ads

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

6. Permissions

Internet Required

Used only to serve ads.

Notifications Optional

Used only to remind you, around 8 pm on the day after a trip, that its receipt is still open. If you do not allow it, everything else in the Service works as before.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly handle personal information from children.

8. Your rights

You can edit or delete any line or receipt inside the Service at any time, and you can erase all data on your device by uninstalling the Service. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Privacy > Ads.

9. Contact

For questions about privacy, please email:

  • Email: axno.app@gmail.com

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page together with its effective date.
